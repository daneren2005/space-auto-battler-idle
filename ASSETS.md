Game Dev GUI Goodness - January 2021 GUI and Icons\spacegameguiset

Ship silhouettes (public/ships/*.png):
Original art for this project - one white top-down silhouette per ship type, tinted
per faction at runtime exactly like public/boid.png. Generated by public/ships/generate.py
(Python + Pillow); re-run it to regenerate the full roster. License: same as this project.

Explosion (public/effects/explosion.png):
Detonator blast sprite, composited by public/effects/generate.py (Python + Pillow + numpy)
from the EVFX Blast MZ effect textures (the soft radial glow, 4-point flash and smoke puff)
over a procedural fire gradient + shock ring. Source: "Game Creator - September 2024" Humble
Bundle, Effects\evfxblast (EVFX Blast MZ). Point generate.py's SRC at your copy to regenerate.
License: EVFX Blast is RPG-Maker-oriented commercial art - re-verify its redistribution terms
before shipping in a web build (see plans/05-assets.md).
